### BizLens — SMB's Decision Support System

**BizLens** is a full-stack business management and analytics platform designed to help Small and Medium-sized Businesses (SMBs) understand their operational performance and make informed decisions.

**🔍 Business Problem**

Business information related to products, inventory, customers, orders, and expenses is often scattered across separate records and spreadsheets. BizLens brings these operational records into a centralized system and transforms them into meaningful business information.

**📊 Key Capabilities**

- **Business KPI Analytics:** Revenue, product costs, gross profit, operating expenses, net profit, profit margin, and average order value.
- **Product & Inventory Management:** Product records, stock monitoring, and inventory updates linked to order transactions.
- **Customer & Order Analysis:** Customer activity, returning-customer classification, order history, and product performance.
- **Business Insights & Alerts:** Rule-based alerts for low stock, low margins, loss-making products, and expense-related concerns.
- **Decision Support:** Period-based performance comparisons, expense analysis, and simplified break-even analysis.
- **Data Export:** Business data export in CSV and supported spreadsheet formats.

**🛠️ Technology Stack**

`HTML` · `CSS` · `JavaScript` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MySQL`
